# Executive scorecard: the 8 KPIs and why

| # | KPI | Metric | Source view | Why a founder needs it | Target / benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Revenue | M16 | m16_revenue | Shows whether the business earns enough to pay for everything else. | Benchmark: 18-month company average (no target in the data) |
| 2 | Operating margin | M18 | m18_operating_margin | One number for: are we making money on what we earn? | Benchmark: 18-month company average (no target in the data) |
| 3 | Payroll as % of revenue | M19 | m19_payroll_pct_revenue | Payroll is the biggest cost; this shows if people cost is outgrowing income. | Benchmark: 18-month company average (no target in the data) |
| 4 | Days sales outstanding | M20 | m20_dso | Profit means little if customers pay late; this is how long cash takes to arrive. | Benchmark: 18-month company average (no target in the data) |
| 5 | Attrition, rolling 12 months | M05 | m01_headcount + m03_exits (M05 formula, all departments) | Losing people is costly and hard to reverse; the dictionary says to use the 12-month view for headlines. | kpi_targets: 1% a month x 12 = 12% |
| 6 | Attendance rate | M13 | m13_attendance_rate (weighted by employees) | Shows the capacity the company really has, and early morale problems. | kpi_targets: 94% |
| 7 | Task completion rate | M22 | m22_task_completion_rate (weighted by tasks) | Shows whether planned work is being delivered. | kpi_targets: 80% |
| 8 | Overtime share | M11 | m11_overtime_share x m09_payroll_cost (weighted by cost) | Overtime signals stretched capacity and extra cost. | kpi_targets, weighted by cost |

## Considered and left out
- Overdue receivables (M21) and high-risk share (M27): only one date in the data, so no trend can be shown.
- Forecast error (M28): empty, because actuals end before the forecast months.
- Anomaly flag rate (M26): direction is "context dependent" and flags are leads, not proof.
- Net profit (M17): tells the same story as operating margin, and margin is easier to compare.
- Headcount (M01): no target, and its direction is "context dependent".

## Status rule
Green = on or better than target. Amber = worse, but within a tolerance band. Red = outside the band.
Bands (my judgement, shown on the page): revenue Rs 5 M, margin 3 pts, payroll % 3 pts, DSO 5 days, attrition 2 pts, attendance 1.5 pts, task completion 5 pts, overtime 0.5 pts.
